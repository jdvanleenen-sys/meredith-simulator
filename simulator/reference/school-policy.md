# Elbow Crossing High School: Academic Honesty Procedure (2025)

4.1 A teacher who has a concern about the authenticity of a student's work first speaks with the student.

4.2 Before any mark consequence is entered, the teacher contacts the parent or guardian of a student who is
not an independent student, and offers a meeting.

4.3 Results from detection software may inform a concern but do not by themselves establish one. The
teacher considers the student's process and other samples of the student's work.

4.4 For a first concern, the student may complete a supervised reassessment of the same outcomes. If the
reassessment shows the learning, its mark replaces the original mark and nothing is placed in the student
record.

4.5 Repeated concerns are referred to administration and may be noted in the student record.

4.6 A student or parent who disagrees with a decision may ask the principal to review it, and may then
appeal to the board under section 42 of the Education Act.
